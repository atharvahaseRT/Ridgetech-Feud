    How to play

    🎲 Random teams

    The host starts and teams are drawn at random. Your captain ★ has 30 s to name the team (hit 🎲 for ideas).

    The host picks the question categories and sets a 5, 7 or 10 minute game clock. Rounds keep coming until it runs low.

    ⚡ Face-off

    One player from each of two teams. Wait for GO, then hit Space or tap the buzzer.

    Jump early and you lose 1 second. Fastest buzz answers first.

    ✍️ Play the board

    The face-off winner's team takes control. Typing rotates: watch for ANSWERING and UP NEXT.

    You get 12 s to answer. Three wrong answers = three strikes.

    🚨 Steal

    After 3 strikes every other team races to buzz in and steal the pot.

    The last round before the bonus pays ×2.

    🌙 Bonus round

    Captains secretly bet RB$. Your team gets 3 guesses: land 2 or more and win +1.5× your bet. Miss and you lose it.

    Most Ridgetech Bucks at the summit wins.
