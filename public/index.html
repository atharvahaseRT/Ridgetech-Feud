    How to play

    1. Teams are assigned automatically when the host starts.
    2. Face-off: one player from each of two teams. Wait for GO, then hit Space (or tap the buzzer). Jumping early costs you 1 second.
    3. The team that wins the face-off plays the board. Typing rotates: only the highlighted typer can answer.
    4. Three wrong answers = 3 strikes. Then every other team races to buzz in and steal the pot.
    5. The last regular round pays double points.
    6. Bonus round: each captain secretly bets RB$. Your team gets 3 guesses. Land 2 or more on the board and win 1.5× your bet. Otherwise you lose the bet.
    7. Scores are Ridgetech Bucks (RB$). Highest climb wins.
